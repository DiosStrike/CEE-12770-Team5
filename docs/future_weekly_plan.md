# Future Weekly Plan

This plan outlines the remaining milestones for our project from April 7, 2026 to May 3, 2026, with the goal of completing the in-person demo, demo video, and final report on time.

## Week 1 (April 7 – April 13)

The main goal of this week is to strengthen the connection between real-world recordings and the anomaly detection pipeline. We will collect additional fan recordings under controlled normal and abnormal conditions, including different voltage settings and blocked airflow scenarios. During the same period, we will continue refining the current demo workflow and verify that both uploaded-audio inference and live microphone inference operate reliably. By the end of the week, we aim to have a larger real-world audio set and a more stable demo interface for further model adaptation.

## Week 2 (April 14 – April 20)

The focus of this week is model adaptation and evaluation. We will fine-tune the baseline model on target-domain normal audio collected from our own fan setup, then compare the model’s behavior before and after adaptation. We will also work on improving consistency between offline recordings and real-time inference, and begin updating thresholds based on the score distribution of the adapted model. In parallel, we will start preparing materials for the poster and in-person demo, including system diagrams, demo screenshots, and preliminary results.

## Week 3 (April 21 – April 27)

This week is centered on demo delivery and communication materials. Before the in-person demo day on **Friday, April 24**, we will finalize the live demo workflow, test the hardware and software setup, and prepare the poster for presentation. After the in-person demo, we will focus on producing and polishing the project demo video, which is due on **Sunday, April 26**. By the end of this week, we aim to complete the poster presentation, finalize the demo video submission, and lock down the main experimental results for the report.

## Week 4 (April 28 – May 3)

The final week is dedicated to completing the written report. We will integrate the final experimental results, refine figures and tables, polish the methodology and discussion sections, and ensure that the full report is consistent and complete. We will also review the final structure, formatting, and references before submission. The final objective of this week is to submit the completed project final report by **Sunday, May 3**.

## Final Deliverables

By the end of this schedule, the team plans to complete the following deliverables:

- an in-person project demo and poster presentation by **April 24**
- a finalized demo video submission by **April 26**
- a completed final written report by **May 3**
